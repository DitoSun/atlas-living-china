Guilin

Where China Inspires

Days 8–10 · Float through a living painting of mist-shrouded peaks and ancient waterways.

The Li River Cruise

This is the China of your dreams — a dream that has been real for poets and painters for over a thousand years. Your family will board a private boat and drift along the Li River, where karst peaks rise from the mist like ancient brushstrokes. Water buffalo wade in shallows, fishermen float by on bamboo rafts with cormorants, and every bend in the river reveals a scene more beautiful than the last. This is not a sight — it's a state of being.

Yangshuo & Karst Landscapes

Yangshuo is where the landscape becomes art — and your family becomes part of it. Cycle through emerald rice paddies with towering limestone peaks as your backdrop. Explore ancient villages where time seems to stand still. Watch farmers tend their fields as they have for generations. This is rural China at its most beautiful, a world away from the bustling cities you've left behind. Your family will breathe deeply here, in every sense.

Cultural Immersion

Guilin Experiences

Countryside Cycling Adventure

Your family will cycle through some of the most beautiful countryside on Earth. Follow paths that wind through rice terraces, past water buffalo, and into villages where farmers still work the land with their hands. This is the China that inspired poets for centuries — and now, your family lives it.

Minority Village Visit

Meet the Zhuang and Yao minority communities who call the karst mountains home. Your family will be welcomed into a village, share a traditional meal, and learn about customs and crafts that have been preserved for generations. A profound lesson in cultural diversity and resilience.

Impression Liu Sanjie

As night falls, witness one of the world's most extraordinary performances — a spectacular outdoor show directed by Zhang Yimou, set against the natural backdrop of the karst mountains. 600 performers on the Li River create a breathtaking fusion of light, music, and nature.

Bamboo Rafting

For a truly serene family moment, take a bamboo raft along the Yulong River. Drift past ancient bridges, weeping willows, and buffalo-cooling pools. The gentle rhythm of the water and the majesty of the surrounding peaks create a memory your family will treasure forever.
