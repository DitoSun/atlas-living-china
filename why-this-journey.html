Who Is This For

This Journey Is Designed For You

Urban French Families

You value design, cuisine, and culture. Your family is curious, well-travelled, and ready for something beyond the typical resort holiday. China offers the depth and sophistication you crave.

Culture Seekers

Museums and monuments are just the beginning. You want to understand the soul of a place — its traditions, its people, its way of life. Every encounter on this journey is designed for meaning.

Food Lovers

For your family, cuisine is the heart of culture. From Beijing's imperial feasts to Xi'an's Silk Road spices, from Shanghai's contemporary dining to Guilin's farm-to-table freshness — your palates will travel as far as your feet.

History Enthusiasts

Not just names and dates — you want to touch history, to walk where emperors walked, to understand how the past shapes the present. China's story is your story. Your family will live it.

Multi-Generational Travellers

Grandparents, parents, teenagers — each generation experiences China differently, yet together. Our journey includes activities that delight every age, from calligraphy for the young to tea ceremonies for the wise.

Lifestyle Seekers

Travel is not just where you go — it's how you live along the way. Our handpicked hotels, private guides, and exclusive access ensure your family travels in comfort and style, without sacrificing authenticity.
