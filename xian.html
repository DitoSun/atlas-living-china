Xi'an

Where China Began

Days 4–5 · Walk in the footsteps of emperors along the Silk Road's legendary eastern terminus.

The Terracotta Warriors

In 1974, farmers digging a well stumbled upon one of the greatest archaeological discoveries of all time — an entire army of life-sized terracotta soldiers, quietly standing guard for over 2,200 years. Your family will stand before thousands of warriors, each with a unique face, in the vast pits where they were discovered. Our archaeologist guide reveals the secrets of Qin Shi Huang's mausoleum, the first emperor's vision of eternity, and the astonishing craftsmanship that created this silent legion.

The Ancient City Wall

Xi'an's city wall is the best-preserved ancient fortification in China — a 14-kilometre embrace that has protected the city for over 600 years. Your family will bicycle along its broad ramparts as the sun sets over the city, watching modern Xi'an unfold below. From this vantage point, you can see the old city within the walls and the new city beyond — a powerful metaphor for China's journey through time.

Cultural Immersion

Xi'an Experiences

Muslim Quarter

Dive into the vibrant heart of Xi'an's Muslim Quarter, where the flavours of the Silk Road come alive. Your family will wander through bustling food stalls, sample lamb skewers sizzling with cumin, taste freshly pulled noodles, and discover the fascinating融合 (fusion) of Chinese and Islamic cultures.

Silk Road Heritage

Xi'an was the magnificent starting point of the Silk Road. Visit the Great Mosque — a serene fusion of Chinese and Islamic architecture — and learn how merchants, missionaries, and ideas flowed between East and West, shaping the world as we know it.

Tang Dynasty Evening

As night falls, Xi'an transforms. Your family will enjoy a Tang Dynasty dinner show — a spectacular performance of music, dance, and costumes that brings the golden age of Chinese poetry and culture to life. A magical experience for all ages.

Calligraphy & Seal Carving

In the shadow of the Wild Goose Pagoda, a master artisan teaches your family the ancient art of seal carving. Each family member creates their own personal chop — a signature stamp that has been used in China for thousands of years — as a lasting memento of your journey.
