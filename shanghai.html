Shanghai

Where China Evolves

Days 6–7 · Experience China's future in a dazzling metropolis where tradition meets innovation.

The Bund & Lujiazui

Stand on the Bund, where colonial-era Art Deco buildings line the Huangpu River, and gaze across at Pudong's futuristic skyline — a forest of towers that seems to have risen from science fiction. This single view encapsulates China's extraordinary journey: the old world meeting the new, tradition embracing innovation. Your family will watch the city light up at dusk, a moment that defines Shanghai's electric spirit.

The French Concession

For French families, the former French Concession offers a fascinating glimpse into a shared history. Tree-lined avenues, elegant villas, and hidden boutiques create a neighbourhood that feels both familiar and distinctly Shanghainese. Your family will explore this district with a local artist, discovering contemporary galleries, speciality tea houses, and the thriving café culture that makes this area the creative heart of modern Shanghai.

Cultural Immersion

Shanghai Experiences

Contemporary Art Discovery

Shanghai is Asia's contemporary art capital. Your family will visit the Power Station of Art — a former power plant turned into one of the world's most innovative museums — and explore the vibrant M50 art district, meeting young Chinese artists shaping the future of global culture.

Tea Culture Masterclass

In a serene tea house hidden in the French Concession, a tea master introduces your family to the ancient art of Chinese tea ceremony. Taste rare oolongs, learn the language of tea, and discover why tea has been at the heart of Chinese culture for thousands of years.

Shanghai Food Safari

As night falls, your family embarks on a culinary journey through Shanghai's diverse neighbourhoods. From xiaolongbao (soup dumplings) in a legendary local restaurant to late-night noodles in a hidden alleyway, every stop reveals a new layer of Shanghai's extraordinary food culture.

Huangpu River Night Cruise

Board a private boat for a sunset cruise along the Huangpu River. As the Bund's colonial architecture lights up on one side and Pudong's neon skyline blazes on the other, your family will witness Shanghai at its most magical — a city of dazzling contrasts, all in a single view.
