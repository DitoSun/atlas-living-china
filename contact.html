Practical Information

Everything You Need to Know

Visa Information

French passport holders require a visa to enter China. Atlas Living provides full visa support, including invitation letters and application guidance. Processing typically takes 4–7 working days. We recommend applying at least 4 weeks before departure.

Currency

The official currency is the Chinese Yuan Renminbi (CNY / ¥). Major credit cards are accepted in hotels and high-end establishments. We recommend carrying some cash for local markets and smaller vendors. ATMs are widely available.

Weather

China's climate varies by region and season. Spring (March–May) and Autumn (September–November) offer the most pleasant temperatures. Our journey is designed for optimal seasonal conditions, with detailed packing guidance provided upon booking.

Payment Methods

Alipay and WeChat Pay are the dominant payment systems in China. Your Atlas Living guide will assist with setup upon arrival. International credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) are accepted at hotels and major restaurants.

Transportation

All internal transportation is included: high-speed trains (Beijing → Xi'an), domestic flights (Xi'an → Shanghai, Shanghai → Guilin), and private transfers throughout. Our guides ensure seamless logistics at every step.

Emergency Contacts

Your Atlas Living guide is available 24/7 throughout the journey. Emergency numbers: 110 (Police), 120 (Ambulance), 119 (Fire). We also provide travel insurance recommendations and keep your family's safety as our highest priority.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is China safe for family travel? +

Absolutely. China is one of the safest countries for family travel, with low crime rates and a strong tourist infrastructure. Our guides are with you at all times, and we choose neighbourhoods and hotels that are well-established for international visitors.

What is the best time of year for this journey? +

Spring (April–May) and Autumn (September–October) offer the most comfortable temperatures and clearest skies across all four cities. Our itineraries are scheduled during these optimal periods.

Will there be language barriers? +

Our English-speaking guides accompany your family throughout the entire journey. In hotels, major restaurants, and tourist sites, English is widely spoken. We also provide a basic Mandarin phrasebook for your family before departure.

What about dietary restrictions? +

We accommodate all dietary needs — vegetarian, halal, gluten-free, and allergies. Our guides communicate with every restaurant in advance, and our hands-on cooking experiences can be adapted to your family's preferences.

Is the journey suitable for teenagers? +

Absolutely. Our experiences are designed to engage young adults — cycling through Yangshuo, exploring the Muslim Quarter's food scene, the Tang Dynasty show, and hands-on workshops like dumpling making and calligraphy. Teenagers leave inspired.

What is your cancellation policy? +

We offer flexible cancellation terms. Full details are provided at booking. We strongly recommend travel insurance to protect your investment. Contact us for our complete terms and conditions.
