Where Comfort Meets Character

Each hotel is handpicked for its location, heritage, and ability to make your family feel truly at home.

"Luxury is not just about comfort — it's about feeling the soul of a place. Every hotel we choose is a destination in itself, chosen for its story, its architecture, and its ability to make your family's journey richer."

The Peninsula Beijing

Beijing · Wangfujing

A masterpiece of Art Deco design in the heart of Beijing, steps from the Forbidden City. Spacious suites, a legendary spa, and service that anticipates your every need.

Forbidden City views Family suites Chinese spa Michelin dining

Why selected: Unbeatable location, family-friendly luxury, and a century of hosting dignitaries.

The Westin Xi'an

Xi'an · Qujiang

A modern sanctuary near the Wild Goose Pagoda, blending Tang Dynasty aesthetics with contemporary luxury. Rooms offer panoramic views of Xi'an's ancient skyline.

Pagoda views Indoor pool Cultural tours Kids' programs

Why selected: Proximity to ancient sites and exceptional family facilities.

The Puli Hotel & Spa

Shanghai · Jing'an

Shanghai's most serene urban retreat, hidden in the former French Concession. Award-winning design, a stunning indoor pool, and a philosophy of understated elegance.

French Concession Urban spa Design award Garden terrace

Why selected: Unparalleled design, calm in the city's heart, and exceptional family service.

Banyan Tree Yangshuo

Guilin · Yangshuo

A breathtaking resort nestled among the karst mountains, where traditional Chinese architecture meets modern luxury. Each villa opens onto views of the Li River and limestone peaks.

Karst mountain views Private villas Li River access Organic garden

Why selected: Spectacular location, authentic design, and immersion in nature without sacrificing luxury.
