Beijing

Where China Remembers

Days 1–3 · Walk through 500 years of imperial history, where every corner tells a story of emperors, artisans, and revolutionaries.

The Forbidden City

The world's largest imperial palace complex is not a museum — it is a city within a city, where 24 emperors once walked. Your family will step through the Meridian Gate into a world of golden roofs, marble balustrades, and courtyards that have witnessed the rise and fall of dynasties. Our expert guide brings the stones to life with stories of court intrigue, imperial ceremonies, and the daily life of the Forbidden City's 9,000 rooms.

Temple of Heaven

A masterpiece of architectural symbolism, the Temple of Heaven is where emperors once performed elaborate rituals to ensure good harvests. Watch Beijing's elders practise tai chi in the surrounding park at dawn, listen to the song of birds in ancient cypress trees, and discover why this UNESCO site is the soul of Beijing. Your family will understand why harmony between heaven and earth is the foundation of Chinese thought.

Cultural Immersion

Beijing Experiences

Hutong Experience

Navigate the maze-like alleyways of old Beijing by rickshaw. Visit a local family in their courtyard home, learn about hutong culture, and discover how modern Beijingers balance tradition with contemporary life.

Peking Duck Ceremony

Not just a meal — a ritual. Watch master carvers slice crispy-skinned duck tableside at one of Beijing's most esteemed restaurants. Your family will learn the art of wrapping duck in thin pancakes with spring onion and sweet bean sauce.

Calligraphy Workshop

In a quiet studio tucked away from the city's bustle, a master calligrapher guides your family through the meditative art of brushstrokes. Each character becomes a shared work of art to take home.

Family Dumpling Making

Gather around a kitchen table in a traditional hutong home. Grandmothers teach your family the art of folding dumplings — jiaozi — while sharing stories of family life in Beijing across generations.
